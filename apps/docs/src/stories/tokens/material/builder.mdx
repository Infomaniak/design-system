# Material Theme Builder Tokens

## What are Material Theme Builder Tokens?

_Material Theme Builder Tokens_ are material-specific design tokens that reference T1 (primitive) values,
used only by the [Material Theme Builder](https://material-foundation.github.io/material-theme-builder) to create Material Design themes.

## How to create a new Material Theme?

Go to the [Material Theme Builder](https://material-foundation.github.io/material-theme-builder) app and fill the colors matching the corresponding Material Design tokens:

<img
  src={MaterialThemeBuilderFillColors}
  alt="material-theme-builder--fill-colors"
/>

Then, click on the following buttons until you reach the export format list:

<img
  src={MaterialThemeBuilderPickFonts}
  alt="material-theme-builder--pick-fonts"
/>

<img
  src={MaterialThemeBuilderExportTheme}
  alt="material-theme-builder--export-theme"
/>

<img
  src={MaterialThemeBuilderExport}
  alt="material-theme-builder--export"
/>

Finally, select your export format and click on the corresponding button:

<img
  src={MaterialThemeBuilderExportFormat}
  alt="material-theme-builder--export-format"
/>

It will download a file with the theme in the requested format.
