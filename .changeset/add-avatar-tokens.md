Added Avatar component tokens (`avatar.*`) matching the Avatar component in Figma: size, border, radius (circle and square), background and content colors (including disabled), initials typography, icon size, hover/pressed overlays (with dedicated image-variant overlays), disabled opacity and stacked-avatar overlap. A new `avatar-size` modifier provides `small` (24px), `medium` (32px), `large` (40px) and `extra-large` (48px, default) sizes. Also added negative spacing tokens `spacing.minus-sm` (-6px) and `spacing.minus-md` (-8px).
