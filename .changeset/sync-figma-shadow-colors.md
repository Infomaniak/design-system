Shadow colors (`color.shadow.{xs,sm,md,lg,xl,2xl}`) are synced with Figma (Token Core): they now use `color.gray.700` at 30% opacity in the light theme (previously `color.gray.400` at 6–14%) and `color.gray.500` at 30% opacity in the dark theme (previously `color.gray.950` at 6–14%), giving more visible elevation. Token names, offsets, blur and spread are unchanged.
