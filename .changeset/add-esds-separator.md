Added the `esds-separator` component, which divides content horizontally or vertically. It supports vertical orientation and can display a label (or any content) between its two lines via its default slot. It exposes `role="separator"` semantics with `aria-orientation`, and can be removed from the accessibility tree with the `decorative` attribute. Its appearance is driven by the design tokens.
